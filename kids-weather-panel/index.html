About the recommendations

Four clothing groups use the Ottawa forecast during school hours (morning bus, outdoor recess and afternoon bus), or daytime on weekends. Jackets, hats and snow pants are removable for indoors. These are approximate guidelines, not medically validated prescriptions: check personal comfort, school rules and official alerts.

Age settings are saved only on this device. Weather data comes from Open-Meteo.

Refreshes every 15 minutes while open, and when revisited or touched after 10 minutes. Weekend forecasts work on demand. Official weather alerts are not included.
